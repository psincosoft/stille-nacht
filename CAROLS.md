# The carols

The 25 carols of Stille Nacht, in the round's order. Only music free everywhere: its makers
(composers and arrangers) died before 1926, and it was first published before 1931. The words are not
used.

Seven carols play in four-part settings from Benjamin Bloomfield's collection of Christmas carols,
which its maker gives to the public domain. The other 18 had no free setting: their melodies are set
in four parts of ours, in the manner of the 19th-century chorale books. From the four parts the
engine makes each of the six voices, by rule.

Where the melodies were read: **M** Musikpiraten e.V., *Singen im Advent* (2013; its LilyPond files,
given to the public domain); **W** the score in the carol's article in the German Wikipedia; **WB** the
score in de.wikibooks' *Liederbuch*; **B** Bloomfield's collection.

The settings of ours: a melody in a key of ours and a plan of its harmony; the alto, tenor and
bass found under it by the rules of the chorale books, striking with each chord and holding it. Ours
too: the paces (two are the hymnals'), the slowing ends and the accompaniments.

Dates and forms checked against scans of old prints at the Internet Archive: Reading, Pennsylvania,
1878; Cincinnati, 1882; Brähmig's *Liederstrauß*, 1897 edition; Basel, 1906.
